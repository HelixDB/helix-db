**Records** is the Explorer's table view: one type at a time, searchable and sortable,
with every record's links and where it came from. An entity's page gathers everything
the load knows about one record.

## Read a type as a table

In the Explorer, select **Records** in the bar. Choose a type in the panel beside the
sidebar, or above the table while the panel is hidden; the count beside it is how many
records the load holds.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/records.png" alt="Records showing the Customer type as a table with currency and email columns, with the Contract, Customer and Invoice types in the panel" />
</Frame>

- **Search** finds records by name or key.
- **Filter by field** keeps the records whose field matches a value.
- Select a column's header to sort by it, and **Columns** to choose which show.
- Pages hold 50 records. Searches and filters count up to 1,000 matches, and paging
  reaches the first 10,000 records of a type.

## Open a record

Select a row to open the record beside the table: its properties, its links to other
records, and **Where it comes from**. A link or record that came from a table names the
source and row; one that came from a document shows the quote, with a link to it in the
document.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/record.png" alt="The Globex Corporation record with its properties, billed_to and signed_with links, and its Stripe row and contract quote" />
</Frame>

Select **Open in graph** to find it in the Explorer, or **Open its page** for the
entity's page.

## An entity's page

An entity's page names the record's type, key and sources, with tabs for its
**Overview** (properties, each with the row or quote that states it), **Facts** (its
links, with their evidence), **Derived** (what the version's rules derive about it, and
why), **Mentions** (where documents name it) and **Needs review** (reviews, and rule
breaks).

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/entity.png" alt="Globex Corporation's page with its key and sources, Ask about this, its Overview, Facts, Derived, Mentions and Needs review tabs, and each property with its source" />
</Frame>

Every page here reads one load. Use the **Load** picker to read another; links between
pages keep it. **Ask about this** asks a question about the record in a drawer; see
[the Analyst](/foundry/ask/ask-questions#ask-about-a-record).

**Derived** shows only for a version with rules. Its facts are worked out when the page
reads them and are never stored; one too costly to work out says so.

## Settle a review

**Needs review**, on an entity's page or a document's, lists what the load couldn't
settle by itself, grouped by reason. Each group gives its title, such as **Names
something that isn't in your data**, **Could mean more than one entity**, **The model
wasn't sure** or **A relationship the ontology doesn't have**, with a count and what it
means, then a card per item. Owners and admins settle them while the page reads the
current load; on another load, a card says **Open the current load to settle this
review**.

| On a card | What it does | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approve** a claim held for a person to check | Writes the link the document's sentence states | To this load at once. If a name in it still needs review, the first load after that name is settled writes it. Later loads read the approval too. |
| **Reject** a held claim | Writes nothing, and takes the card out of Needs review | In this load and later ones |
| **Keep value**, or **Replace the table's value with** one a document states | Keeps one of the values documents disagree on, or overrides a table's value | Sent to this load at once, and later loads read it too. A chosen value holds even after the table changes; a document dated after your decision still wins. |
| **Link this name to** a record, or **Not in data** | Settles which record a name means, or that none does | From the next load. Load again to see it. |

A link that holds one value, such as a contract's single customer, says on its card what
an approval replaces. Approve those one at a time.

Two group actions save work:

- **Approve all (N)**, on a group of held claims, approves every claim in it that can be
  written now: this load writes each one it can at once, and any it can't stays held,
  with its card saying why. Claims of a link that holds one value are left out.
- **Add these links**, on **A relationship the ontology doesn't have**, opens
  [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#generate-a-draft) with a goal
  asking for the links those reviews suggest. It drafts a new version for you to review;
  the current version stays until you choose otherwise.

**Saved decisions** lists every decision. Removing one undoes it: a value goes back out
of the current load at once, a rejected claim is listed again, and a name decision
applies from the next load.
