**Records** is the Explorer's table view: one type at a time, searchable and sortable,
with every record's links and where it came from. An entity's page gathers everything
the load knows about one record.

## Read a type as a table

In the Explorer, select **Records**. Choose a type at the top; the count beside it is
how many records the load holds.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/records.png" alt="Records showing the Customer type as a table with currency and email columns" />
</Frame>

- **Search** finds records by name or key.
- **Filter by field** keeps the records whose field matches a value.
- Select a column's header to sort by it, and **Columns** to choose which show.
- Pages hold 50 records. Searches and filters count up to 1,000 matches, and paging
  reaches the first 10,000 records of a type.

## Open a record

Select a row to open the record beside the table: its properties, its links to other
records, and **Where it comes from**. A link or record that came from a table names the
source and row; one that came from a document shows the quote, with a link to it in the
document.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/record.png" alt="The Globex Corporation record with its properties, billed_to and signed_with links, and its Stripe row and contract quote" />
</Frame>

Select **Open in graph** to find it in the Explorer, or **Open its page** for the
entity's page.

## An entity's page

An entity's page names the record's type, key and sources, with tabs for its
**Overview** (properties, each with the row or quote that states it), **Facts** (its
links, with their evidence), **Derived** (what the version's rules derive about it, and
why), **Mentions** (where documents name it) and **Needs review** (reviews, and rule
breaks).

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/entity.png" alt="Globex Corporation's page with its key, sources and properties" />
</Frame>

Every page here reads one load. Use the **Load** picker to read another; links between
pages keep it. **Ask about this** asks a question about the record in a drawer; see
[Ask](/foundry/ask/ask-questions#ask-about-a-record).

**Derived** shows only for a version with rules. Its facts are worked out when the page
reads them and are never stored; one too costly to work out says so.

## Settle a review

Owners and admins settle a review in **Needs review** on an entity's page or a
document's, while it reads the current load: link a name to the record it means or mark
it **Not in data**, keep one of the values documents disagree on, or replace a table's
value with one a document states. A decision applies from the next load, so load again
to see it; a value chosen over a table's holds in every later load, even after the
table changes. **Saved decisions** lists the decisions, and removing one also applies
from the next load.
