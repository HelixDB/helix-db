**Records** is the Explorer's table view: one type at a time, searchable and sortable,
with every record's links and where it came from. An entity's page gathers everything
the load knows about one record. For why every value names its source, see
[What is data provenance?](/learn/ontologies/what-is-data-provenance)

## Read a type as a table

In the Explorer, select **Records** in the bar. Choose a type in the panel beside the
sidebar, or above the table while the panel is hidden; the count beside it is how many
records the load holds.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/records.png" alt="Records showing the Customer type as a table with currency and email columns, with the Contract, Customer and Invoice types in the panel" />
</Frame>

- **Search** finds records by name or key.
- **Filter by field** keeps the records whose field matches a value.
- Select a column's header to sort by it, and **Columns** to choose which show.
- Pages hold 50 records. Searches and filters count up to 1,000 matches, and paging
  reaches the first 10,000 records of a type.

## Open a record

Select a row to open the record beside the table: its properties, its links to other
records, and **Where it comes from**. A link or record that came from a table names the
source and row; one that came from a document shows the quote, with a link to it in the
document.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/record.png" alt="The Globex Corporation record with its properties, billed_to and signed_with links, and its Stripe row and contract quote" />
</Frame>

Select **Open in graph** to find it in the Explorer, or **Open its page** for the
entity's page. If you're [restricted to some sources](/foundry/workspace/access#restricted-members),
Records shows only the records you can read, and a record has no **Open in graph**.

## An entity's page

An entity's page names the record's type, key and sources, with tabs for its
**Overview** (properties, each with the row or quote that states it), **Facts** (its
links, with their evidence), **Derived** (what the version's rules derive about it, and
why), **Mentions** (where documents name it) and **Needs review** (reviews, and rule
breaks).

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/explore/entity.png" alt="Globex Corporation's page with its key and sources, Ask about this, its Overview, Facts, Derived, Mentions and Needs review tabs, and each property with its source" />
</Frame>

Every page here reads one load. Use the **Load** picker to read another; links between
pages keep it. **Ask about this** opens a new chat about the record, with your message already
started as `About <record>: `. Finish the question and send it to
[Founder](/foundry/ask/founder); see [Ask about a record](/foundry/ask/ask-questions#ask-about-a-record).

**Derived** shows only for a version with rules. Its facts are worked out when the page
reads them and are never stored; one too costly to work out says so.

## Settle a review

**Needs review**, on an entity's page or a document's, lists what the load couldn't
settle by itself, as a card per item, grouped by reason with a count and what it means,
such as **Names something that
isn't in your data**, **Could mean more than one entity**, **The model wasn't sure** or
**A relationship the ontology doesn't have**. Owners and admins settle them while
the page reads the current load; on another load, a card says **Open the current load to
settle this review**.

| On a card | What it does | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approve** a claim held for a person to check | Writes the link the document's sentence states | This load at once, and later loads. If a name in it still needs review, this load keeps it held until that name is settled, then writes it. |
| **Reject** a held claim | Writes nothing, and takes the card out of Needs review | This load and later ones |
| **Keep value**, or **Replace the table's value with** one a document states | Keeps one of the values documents disagree on, or overrides a table's value | This load at once, and later loads. A chosen value holds even after the table changes; a document dated after your decision still wins. |
| **Link this name to** a record, or **Not in data**, then **Settle review** | Settles which record a name means (here and in other documents where nothing else links it), or that none does (this document only) | This load at once, and later loads. On a name the load already linked, a new choice or **Undo link** applies from the next load. |

A link that holds one value, such as a contract's single customer, says on its card what
an approval replaces. Approve those one at a time.

On a group:

- **Approve all (N)**, on held claims, writes every claim it can at once; any it can't
  stays held, with its card saying why. It leaves out claims of a link that holds one
  value.
- **Add these links**, on **A relationship the ontology doesn't have**, opens
  [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#generate-a-draft) with a goal
  asking for the links those reviews suggest. The new version waits for your review;
  the current one stays until you choose otherwise.

To work through every open review of the current load in one place, open
[Review](/foundry/ontologies/review) under the ontology in the Ontologies panel; its count is how many are open. It lists the
decisions that write the most facts first, with the same controls as a card. There is no
**Approve all** there.

**Saved decisions** lists the decisions for this document or record. **Remove decision**
undoes one: a value leaves the current load at once, a rejected claim is listed again,
and a name decision stays in this load but later loads won't read it. **Take back
approval** takes an approval out of the current load at once, and its claim is held for
review again.
