The Explorer draws what a load wrote into the ontology's database: its records and how
they connect. It reads the database with your session and never writes to it. Switch
to **Records** in the bar at the top to read the same data as tables.

## Read the graph

The Explorer opens on a connected sample of the current load: records of its central
types, their neighbors, and the links among them. **Labels**, the panel beside the
rail, lists the version's types and links with how many the load holds and how many are
drawn. The slim bar above the graph holds the **Records** and **Graph** switch, the
**Ontology**, **Load** and **Compare with** pickers, **Jump to a node**, what the graph
holds, reload and the shortcuts.

| To | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Jump to a record by name, id or text | Press <kbd>/</kbd> and type, then <kbd>Enter</kbd> |
| Load a record's neighbors | Double-click it, or use **Expand** in the inspector |
| Draw more of a type | Select **+** beside it in Labels, 50 records at a time |
| See every action on a record | Right-click it |
| Hide or show the inspector | Press <kbd>]</kbd>, or use its toggle in the bar |
| Hide or show Labels | Press <kbd>[</kbd>, or use the toggle on the rail |
| See every shortcut | Press <kbd>?</kbd> |

The inspector is the sidebar on the right. With nothing selected, it gives an overview
of the database. Select a record to open it there: its type, its properties, the load
that wrote it, and its relationships with their counts. Foundry remembers whether the
inspector is shown, per database.

On an entity's page, in a compare and while the graph loads, the panel shows the
ontology, its database, the load you're reading and links to the Graph and Records. On
a phone, Labels is in the navigation drawer, and the inspector opens as a sheet.

Records are named the way the version says: by a name property, else by their keys.
A record drawn with a dashed outline was reached outside the load you're reading; the
inspector names its load.

## Choose a load

The **Load** picker reads another load of the ontology. Links between the Explorer,
Records, a record's page and a document keep the load you chose. The **Ontology**
picker beside it opens another ontology's Explorer; **Explorer** in the sidebar or on
the rail opens the ontology you last opened.

**Other data** shows what no load wrote: data the database held already, or that your
own applications write. It is hidden while you read a load, and listed below the labels.

## Show Foundry's own records

Loads also write Foundry's bookkeeping beside your data: documents, their sections,
mentions and evidence, and reviews. The Explorer hides these until you select **Show
Foundry internals** under Labels. They are then listed apart and drawn grey. The choice
is kept in this browser.

## Compare two loads

Choose a second load under **Compare with**, or select **Compare with current** in a
load's menu in [Data loads](/foundry/ontologies/load-data#manage-loads). The two loads
are drawn side by side, each with its own bar, labels and inspector, over **What
changed**: the count of each type and link in each load. Open a type to see the
records added, removed and renamed between them, by key, and select one to find it on
its side.

## When a database is starting

A database created in the last few minutes may not answer yet. The Explorer says it is
starting and opens by itself once it answers.
