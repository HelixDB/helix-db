Reading documents into loads and answering questions use AI models, so they cost money;
reading tables doesn't. Each has a monthly cap per workspace, the same on every plan.
Select your name at the foot of the sidebar: **Usage**, in the account menu, shows what
the workspace has spent this month.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/workspace/usage.png" alt="The account menu's Usage: this month's total, then Loads of $50.00, API sources within it of $20.00, and Questions of $15.00, each with a bar" />
</Frame>

| Row | What it counts | Cap each month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This month** | Everything below, once | |
| **Loads** | Reading documents, and API sources' pieces, into your ontologies' loads | $50 |
| **API sources** | Part of Loads: extracting API sources' pieces that no one started a load for | $20 |
| **Questions** | Answering questions in Ask and through the Helix MCP | $15 |

Each row's bar fills as the month's spend nears its cap. Point at a row for what it
counts; point at the total for the day the caps start again, the first of each month
(UTC). Everyone in the workspace sees it. Spend on the workspace's
[own model key](/foundry/workspace/ai-models) counts toward the caps too.

## When a cap is reached

| Cap | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A load: $10 | The load stops **Partial**, with its tables written. The documents it didn't reach are read into it once it's current and the month has budget. |
| Loads: $50 a month | New loads are refused, and new documents aren't read, until next month. Work no person started, such as [refreshes](/foundry/ontologies/load-data#automatic-refreshes) and reading documents as they arrive, stops at $40, which leaves the last $10 for loads you start. |
| API sources: $5 per source, $20 a month | Extraction no person started stops for the month: new pieces wait for next month, and ontologies keep the pieces they have. |
| A question: $0.50 | The question stops at its limit. Ask something narrower. |
| Questions: $15 a month | A question waits until the month's budget can hold it, or you cancel it. |

A refresh that runs with the month spent still writes your tables, and reuses what
earlier loads read from documents at no cost.

Drafting an ontology isn't in Usage: each generation attempt stops at $5.
