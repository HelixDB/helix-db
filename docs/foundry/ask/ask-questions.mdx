The **Analyst**, Foundry's main page, answers questions about an ontology's load in
plain language. Foundry's agent reads the load from the ontology's database step by
step, and its answer cites each record, table row, quote and rule it used, so you can
open every one.

## Start a thread

Foundry opens on the Analyst, and **Analyst** in the sidebar comes back to it. It opens
the ontology you last opened, or the newest; the **Ontology** picker at the top of the
page opens another. A new thread asks what you want to know about the ontology, with
the composer in the middle of the page and, when Foundry has read the load's version,
the version's questions as suggestions.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ask/new-thread.png" alt="A new Analyst thread: What do you want to know about Customer contracts?, the composer, two suggested questions, and the Load picker in the top bar" />
</Frame>

Write the question, or select a suggestion, then select the arrow or press
<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> on Windows and Linux).
A question is at most 4,000 characters.

The Analyst reads one load: the current one, unless you choose another in the **Load**
picker in the top bar. A load can be asked about once it is **Ready** or **Partial**.
While the ontology has no load, a new thread says **Nothing loaded yet** in place of the
composer, with **Load data** or, while no version is current, **Finish setting up**.

A workspace with no ontology opens on [Get started](/foundry/start-here/quickstart)
instead. A [restricted member](/foundry/workspace/access) who holds no sources yet sees
**Nothing shared with you yet**.

## Read an answer

A thread is one column: each question, then its steps as they run, then the answer,
with the composer pinned at the bottom.

- **Steps** say what the agent read. Its first step reads what the load holds: its
  types and links and how many of each. Then it finds entities, reads them and their
  evidence, runs queries written as a path through the ontology's types and links, and,
  for what those can't say, queries the graph directly with a Helix read. Each step's
  JSON is behind a disclosure. A step that failed says why, and the agent carries on.
- **The answer** is plain text with numbered citations, then **Sources**: each record,
  fact, derived fact, table row, document quote, rule break or step it cites. Select one
  to open it: an entity's page, the row's table, the quote in its document, or the step.
- **Results** of its queries show by their shape: a count or a total as one figure,
  groups as bars (or a line over dates), rows as a table. **Chart** and **Table** switch
  a chart between the two. Tables sort by any column, link each record they name to its
  page, and show their first rows, with **Show all … rows** for the rest.

While a question runs, **Cancel** stops it.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ask/thread.png" alt="A thread asking Who signed the services agreement?, with its last steps, an answer citing eight sources, the follow-up composer, and two threads under Today" />

Follow up in the same thread: the composer at the bottom asks with the thread's earlier
answers. A thread takes one question at a time, so a follow-up waits until the answer
before it is ready.

## Threads

While the Analyst is open, the sidebar lists the ontology's threads under the sections,
by their first question, grouped **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. A thread
being answered says **Answering…**. **New thread** starts another. What questions have
spent this month is under **Usage** in the [account menu](/foundry/workspace/usage).

On a phone, the Analyst shows the threads first; choose one, or **New thread**, to open
it.

## Getting into production

The **Getting into production** card, above the conversation, takes you from trying
Foundry to your own app or agent querying an ontology:

1. **Create an ontology**: done once the workspace has one of its own, not only the
   [sample company's](/foundry/start-here/sample-company). Owners and admins get **New
   ontology** here.
2. **Query it through the Helix MCP**: **Copy prompt** copies a prompt for Claude Code,
   Codex or another agent to add the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp), query this
   ontology and help wire it into your app.

Select the card's head to minimise it; this browser remembers. Members reading through
[shared sources](/foundry/workspace/access) don't see it.

## Ask about a record

On an entity's page, select **Ask about this** to ask about that record in a drawer.
The question is about the entity and the load the page reads; **Open in Analyst** opens
its thread.

## When the Analyst can't answer

| What the thread says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **This ontology can't answer that** | What the load holds doesn't say. The Analyst says why, and what the ontology lacks. **Draft a new version with this** opens [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version) with a goal written from the question. |
| **Stopped at this question's limit** | The question used its budget, steps or time before it could answer. Ask something narrower. |
| **It couldn't answer** | It stopped with an error, which it names. |
| **An attempt failed. Helix will try again** | A passing failure; the question runs again by itself. |
| **Waiting for this month's questions budget** | The month's budget can't hold what a question may spend. It runs once it can, or you can cancel it. |

## Limits and spending

The Analyst sends your question, and what each step reads from the load, to the
workspace's [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models). Questions have their
own monthly budget, apart from loads'; [Usage](/foundry/workspace/usage) shows both.
Your agent can ask too, through the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp#ask-questions),
within the same limits.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per question | $0.50, 20 steps and 4 minutes |
| Questions running per workspace | 2; others wait, queued |
| Questions per workspace, per hour | 30 |
| Per workspace, per month | $15 |
| Question | 4,000 characters |

## Who can ask

Anyone in the workspace who can read the ontology's database: members need **Query
read** on the database's Helix project, or sources shared with them under
[Access](/foundry/workspace/access). New members have neither
([Members and roles](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles#what-new-members-can-read)).
The Analyst only reads the load; it never writes to the database.
