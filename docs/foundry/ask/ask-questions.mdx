Ask [Founder](/foundry/ask/founder), Foundry's main page, a question about your data
in plain language. It asks the ontology's load for you step by step, and its answer
cites each record, table row, quote and rule it used, so you can open every one. This
page covers asking, reading an answer and the limits on questions. For Founder's chats,
its dock, suggestions and the changes it can make, see [Founder](/foundry/ask/founder).

## Ask a question

Foundry opens on Founder, and **Founder** in the sidebar comes back to it. It opens
the ontology you last opened, or the newest; the **Ontology** picker at the top of the
page opens another. A new chat asks what you want to know about the ontology, with the
composer in the middle and, below it, suggestions in four tabs: **Questions**,
**Dashboards**, **Ontologies** and **Workflows**. Select a card to put its prompt in the
composer, then edit it or send it. You can also ask from Founder's dock on any other page
(<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> on Windows and Linux); its
answers show the same way.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ask/new-thread.png" alt="A new Analyst thread: What do you want to know about Customer contracts?, the composer, two suggested questions, and the Load picker in the top bar" />
</Frame>

Write the question, or pick a suggestion, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or select the
arrow (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> adds a new line).
A question is at most 4,000 characters.

Founder reads one load: the current one, unless you choose another in the **Load**
picker in the top bar. A load can be asked about once it is **Ready** or **Partial**.
While the ontology has no load, a new chat says nothing is loaded yet: Founder can't
answer questions about the data until it is, but it can help you load it.

A workspace with no ontology opens on [Get started](/foundry/start-here/quickstart)
instead. A [restricted member](/foundry/workspace/access) who holds no sources yet sees
**Nothing shared with you yet**.

## Read an answer

A thread is one column: each question, then its steps as they run, then the answer,
with the composer pinned at the bottom.

Founder asks the data for you. Its **Asked the data** step holds the cited answer, shown
as below, and **Open the full answer** opens it on its own page. Founder may add a
sentence around the answer, but never rewrites it.

- **Steps** say what the agent read. Its first step reads what the load holds: its
  types and links and how many of each. Then it finds entities, reads them and their
  evidence, runs queries written as a path through the ontology's types and links, and,
  for what those can't say, queries the graph directly with a Helix read. Each step's
  JSON is behind a disclosure. A step that failed says why, and the agent carries on.
- **The answer** is plain text with numbered citations, then **Sources**: each record,
  fact, derived fact, table row, document quote, rule break or step it cites. Select one
  to open it: an entity's page, the row's table, the quote in its document, or the step.
- **Results** of its queries show by their shape: a count or a total as one figure,
  groups as bars (or a line over dates), rows as a table. **Chart** and **Table** switch
  a chart between the two. Tables sort by any column, link each record they name to its
  page, and show their first rows, with **Show all … rows** for the rest. **Add to
  dashboard**, on a chart or table in the answer, puts it on a
  [dashboard](/foundry/automate/dashboards) as a tile. Members reading through shared
  sources don't get it.

While a question runs, **Cancel** under it stops it.

Follow up in the same chat: Founder keeps the conversation, so a follow-up can build on
earlier answers. While Founder works, the composer's send button is **Stop**, which ends
its turn.

## Threads

The sidebar lists your chats with Founder under the sections, grouped **Today**,
**Previous 7 days** and **Older**. **New chat** starts another. Below them, **Earlier
questions** lists the ontology's questions on their own, read only (a member reading
through [shared sources](/foundry/workspace/access) sees only their own); one still
being answered says **Answering…**. What questions have
spent this month is under **Usage** in the [account menu](/foundry/workspace/usage).

On a phone, Founder shows the threads first; choose one, or **New chat**, to open it.

## Getting into production

The **Getting into production** card, above the conversation, takes you from trying
Foundry to your own app or agent querying an ontology:

1. **Create an ontology**: done once the workspace has one of its own, not only the
   [sample company's](/foundry/start-here/sample-company). Owners and admins get **New
   ontology** here.
2. **Query it through the Helix MCP**: **Copy prompt** copies a prompt for Claude Code,
   Codex or another agent to add the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp), query this
   ontology and help wire it into your app.

Select the card's head to minimise it; this browser remembers. Members reading through
[shared sources](/foundry/workspace/access) don't see it.

## Ask about a record

On an entity's page, select **Ask about this** to start a new chat with "About
&lt;name&gt;: " already in the composer; finish the question and send it.

## When Founder can't answer

| What the thread says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **This ontology can't answer that** | What the load holds doesn't say. Founder says why, and what the ontology lacks. **Draft a new version with this** opens [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version) with a goal written from the question. |
| **Stopped at this question's limit** | The question used its budget, steps or time before it could answer. Ask something narrower. |
| **It couldn't answer** | It stopped with an error, which it names. |
| **An attempt failed. Helix will try again** | A passing failure; the question runs again by itself. |
| **Waiting for this month's questions budget** | Questions already running hold what is left of the month's budget. It runs once the month can hold what a question may spend, or you can cancel it. |

## Limits and spending

Founder sends your question, and what each step reads from the load, to the
workspace's [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models). Questions have their
own monthly budget, apart from loads'; [Usage](/foundry/workspace/usage) shows both.
Your agent can ask too, through the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp#ask-questions),
within the same limits.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per question | $0.50, 20 steps and 4 minutes |
| Questions running per workspace | 2; others wait, queued |
| Questions per workspace, per hour | 30 |
| Per workspace, per month | $15 |
| Per member or agent key reading through [shared sources](/foundry/workspace/access) | $10 a month and 10 questions an hour, within the workspace's |
| Question | 4,000 characters |

A new question is refused once the hour's 30 are asked, or once the month's spend leaves
less than a question's $0.50.

Founder's own turns have a budget of their own, $30 a month apart from questions'; when
one runs out, Founder says which. See [Limits](/foundry/reference/limits#founder).

## Who can ask

Anyone in the workspace who can read the ontology's database: members need **Query
read** on the database's Helix project, or sources shared with them under
[Access](/foundry/workspace/access). New members have neither
([Members and roles](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles#what-new-members-can-read)).
Founder only reads the load to answer; it never writes to the database.
