**Founder** is [Foundry's](/foundry/start-here/introduction) assistant. Ask it about your data and it answers with cited
sources. Ask it to do something, such as draft a version, load data or build a
dashboard, and it prepares the change for you to run. Your chats with Founder are your
own: no one else in the workspace sees them. The questions Founder asks your data for
you are the ontology's, though: others who can read it see them, and their answers,
under **Earlier questions**.

## Where to find it

- **Founder's page.** Foundry opens on Founder, the first section in the sidebar. It
  works on one ontology at a time: the **Ontology** picker at the top opens another, and
  the **Load** picker chooses the load it answers from. It starts on the current load.
- **The dock.** On every other page, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
  <kbd>J</kbd> on Windows and Linux), select **Founder** at the foot of the sidebar, or
  choose **Open Founder** in the command palette. On a wide screen it opens as a column
  right of the page and stays open as you move around. On a narrower screen it opens as
  a drawer.

The page and the dock share your chats: a chat you open on Founder's page is the one the
dock shows on other pages.

Each message also sends the page you're on, shown above the composer as a chip such as
**Looking at: Customer contracts / v3 load**. Select the chip's **×** to leave the page
out.

## Chats

**New chat**, at the top of the sidebar's threads, starts a chat. Your chats are listed
under it, grouped **Today**, **Previous 7 days** and **Older**. In the dock, the menu
with the chat's title switches chats, starts a **New thread** or deletes the open one
(**Delete this thread**), along with the actions it prepared.

A new chat asks what you want to know about the ontology. Under the composer,
**Suggestions** offers prompts written for that ontology in up to four tabs:
**Questions**, **Dashboards**, **Ontologies** and **Workflows**. Select a card to put its prompt in the
composer. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Write your message, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> adds
a new line). A message is at most 4,000 characters. While Founder works, **Stop** ends
its reply. You can send again once it has finished.

**Earlier questions**, below your chats, lists the ontology's newest questions: earlier
ones from before Founder, and those Founder or agents asked (a restricted member sees
only their own). They are read only: to go on from one, ask Founder in a new chat.

On an entity's page, **Ask about this** opens a new chat with "About &lt;name&gt;: "
already in the composer.

## How Founder answers

For a question about your data, Founder asks the ontology's load for you. Its
**Asked the data** step holds the answer, with numbered citations, charts and tables.
Founder may add a sentence around it, but never rewrites it. **Open the full answer**
opens it on a page of its own. [Read an answer](/foundry/ask/ask-questions) explains the
steps, citations and results.

Founder's other steps collapse to their names; open one to see what it read. One
message asks the data at most 3 questions, and each of its model calls runs at most 8
tool calls.

From a chart or table in an answer, **Add to dashboard** adds it as a tile to a
dashboard you can change, or to a new one. See [Dashboards](/foundry/automate/dashboards).

When Founder needs more from you, it asks under **Questions for you**: up to four
questions, then **Send answers**. When a draft is waiting on its agent's questions,
Founder can bring them into the chat. **Send answers to the draft** sends your answers
straight to the generation, as answering on its page does
([When the agent asks you](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#when-the-agent-asks-you)).

To add a connection, Founder links to its form, such as **Connect Slack**. You enter
credentials in Foundry's form, never in the chat.

## Changes wait for your click

Founder doesn't change your workspace by itself. It prepares each change as an action
card: what it does, what it acts on, what to check, and the full request. The card's
button names what it does, such as **Create**, **Generate**, **Make current**, **Load**
or **Sync**. Nothing runs until you select it, and it runs as you.

- **Dismiss** drops the card without running anything.
- A card expires, and says when. **Refresh** prepares the same request again.
- **Not named by you** means Founder chose what the card acts on. Check it before you
  run it.
- A chat holds at most 3 cards waiting for you.

Founder can prepare these changes:

| Area | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Ontologies | Create an ontology, generate a draft, revise a version, make a version current |
| Loads | Load, make another load current |
| Data | Sync a connection, add the [sample company's](/foundry/start-here/sample-company) data, create, rename or describe a document set, move a document |
| Review | Settle a [review](/foundry/ontologies/review) |
| Dashboards | Create, change, pin, publish, unpublish or delete a dashboard |
| Workflows | Create, change, run, pause, resume or delete a [workflow](/foundry/automate/workflows). The card shows the steps your click approves. |

### New dashboards you ask for

One change runs without a card. When your message has the word "dashboard" or
"dashboards" in it and Founder makes a new dashboard, it makes it at once, as you,
unpinned and unpublished. Its card reads **Made the dashboard "…"**, with **Open the
dashboard** and **Undo**, which deletes it. Changing, pinning, publishing or deleting a
dashboard is always a card.

## Who can use it

Everyone signed in to the workspace can chat with Founder. Agent keys can't; agents ask
through the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp#ask-questions) instead.

| Who | What Founder does for them |
| --- | --- |
| Owners and admins | Answers, and prepares every change above |
| Members | Answers, and prepares creating, changing or deleting dashboards and workflows (not pinning or publishing). For anything else, it says where in Foundry an owner or admin does it. |
| [Restricted members](/foundry/workspace/access#restricted-members) | Answers about the data shared with them, and changes nothing: no cards, no **Load** picker, no **Add to dashboard**. A chat shows only while they still hold every source it used. |

Founder reads only what you can read. To answer questions about an ontology, you need
to be able to read its database ([Members and roles](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles#what-new-members-can-read)).

## Limits

Founder's replies have a budget of their own, apart from questions'.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per message | $0.50, 20 model calls and 4 minutes |
| Message | 4,000 characters |
| Messages per person, per hour | 30 |
| Replies running per workspace | 2; others wait |
| Spend per workspace, per month | $30 |
| Cards waiting per chat | 3 |

When a limit is reached:

- A reply that reaches its message's step or time limit stops and says so. Ask it to go
  on. One that reaches its $0.50 says it stopped at the message's spending cap.
- After 30 messages in an hour, new messages are refused until the hour has passed.
- Once the month's spend leaves less than one message's $0.50, new messages are refused
  until the first of next month (UTC).
- The questions Founder asks for you count toward the
  [questions' limits](/foundry/reference/limits#questions), not Founder's. When those are
  spent, Founder says that questions were refused.

Founder's spend isn't in [Usage](/foundry/workspace/usage). Every limit is on
[Limits](/foundry/reference/limits).

## AI models

Founder runs on the workspace's **Questions** model in
[Settings › AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models): the default, or the workspace's own
Anthropic key on Pro or Max. It sends your messages, and what its steps read, to that
model. Founder needs a model that calls tools. If the model chosen can't, the composer
says so and Founder can't send until an owner changes it in Settings › AI models; drafts,
loads and questions still run.
