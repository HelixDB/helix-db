The sample company runs Foundry end to end on Acme Corp, a made-up company that sells
scheduling software to field-service businesses: Foundry connects its database and
documents, builds an ontology, loads it and answers a question, all on one page. Owners
and admins can start it.

## Start the sample

On **Get started**, select **See it on a sample company (1 min)** under **Start with a
sample company**. **Acme Corp, a sample company** opens and runs each step by itself;
each step opens as its work finishes and stays open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Acme Corp's data">
    Foundry connects Acme Corp's PostgreSQL database with a read-only login and syncs
    `accounts`, `contacts`, `subscriptions`, `invoices`, `support_tickets` and
    `product_usage`. It uploads nine documents into a document set named **Acme Corp
    sample**: three customer contracts as PDFs, call notes, a support escalation, an
    incident report and a price list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The goal">
    Understand each customer account with its people, plan, contract terms, invoices,
    tickets, usage and calls, to see which renewals are at risk.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The ontology">
    Foundry creates **Acme Corp customers** on a new Helix database from a ready-made
    draft, so there's no AI draft to wait for, makes it current and shows its types and
    links.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Loading">
    The load writes the rows as records, each linked to its account, subscription or
    contact, then reads the documents with AI models for the names and facts they hold.
    **See the load** opens its page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask the Analyst">
    Once the load is written, the page asks the first suggested question and shows the
    answer with the records and documents it came from. Then:

    - under **Ask another**, a suggestion opens its thread in the
      [Analyst](/foundry/ask/ask-questions);
    - **Ask your own question** opens a new thread;
    - **See the records** opens the [Explorer](/foundry/explore/explorer).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Now connect your own data">
    **Connect your own data** opens the [first-ontology steps](/foundry/start-here/quickstart)
    for your own sources. **Set up with your agent** copies a prompt for your agent to do
    them with you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a step fails, the page says why, with **Try again**. Reopening or reloading the page
carries on where it was and makes nothing twice.

## While you explore the sample

While the sample is the workspace's only ontology, every page shows the banner **Sample
data: you're exploring Acme Corp, a made-up company.** For owners and admins it links
**Back to getting started**, or, once the sample's question is answered, **Get started
with your own data**.

The sample's connection and document set show in **Connectors**, but your own
ontologies never offer them as sources. Its ontology, database and load are ordinary:
the Analyst, the Explorer and Records read them like yours.

## What it uses

- **A Helix database** on Helix's shared cluster, in the workspace's first Helix project
  or a new project named after the workspace, on the workspace's
  [Helix Cloud plan](/foundry/workspace/plans-and-billing#the-helix-cloud-plan-for-databases).
- **AI models**: reading the documents and answering questions use the workspace's
  [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models) and count toward its
  [monthly caps](/foundry/workspace/usage) like any other load or question.

Foundry [emails you once](/foundry/start-here/workspaces-and-navigation#emails-from-foundry)
a day later if the workspace still has no data of its own.

## Remove the sample

Delete the **Acme Corp customers** ontology from its overview, then its connection and
documents in **Connectors**.
