Use this page to sign in, move between workspaces and get around Foundry quickly.
Foundry's workspaces are your Helix Cloud workspaces: the same names, members and
roles.

## Sign in

Continue with Google, GitHub or Microsoft. Foundry signs you in with your Helix
account, so use the same provider you use for Helix Cloud.

Password and single sign-on sign-ins aren't offered on Foundry yet. If your company
signs in with single sign-on, Foundry says so; use the
[Helix dashboard](https://helix-db.com/login) instead. If your email isn't verified
with the provider, verify it there or continue with another provider.

## Choose or create a workspace

With one workspace, Foundry opens it. With several, or none, it opens the workspace
picker. Select a workspace to open it, or **Create workspace** to start one. A new
workspace starts on Foundry Free.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/workspaces.png" alt="The workspace picker listing the user's workspaces with a Create workspace button" />
</Frame>

To switch later, use the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar. Foundry remembers
the workspace you chose in this browser.

A workspace without an ontology opens on **Get started**: try the
[sample company](/foundry/start-here/sample-company), hand the setup to your agent, or
create the [first ontology](/foundry/ontologies/create-an-ontology#create-your-first-ontology)
from your own data. Until the workspace has an ontology, the sidebar holds only the
workspace menu, **Get started** and your account, with no search or Settings link; the
account menu offers **Plan & billing**, usage, the theme and **Sign out**. Members see
**This workspace isn't set up yet** until an owner or admin creates one.

## The main page: Analyst

Foundry opens on the [Analyst](/foundry/ask/ask-questions), on the ontology you last
opened, or the newest. Ask your data a question there, or pick a suggestion; the
**Ontology** picker at the top opens another ontology. Select **helixfoundry** at the
top of the sidebar to come back to it from anywhere.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/home.png" alt="The Analyst, Foundry's main page: What do you want to know about Customer contracts? with suggested questions, beside the sidebar with Analyst, Ontologies, Explorer and Connectors, and two threads under New thread" />

## The sidebar

The sidebar is on every page. From the top:

- **helixfoundry**, which opens the Analyst, and the search button.
- The workspace menu, to switch workspaces.
- The sections, by name: **Analyst**, **Ontologies**, **Explorer** and **Connectors**.
- What the open section adds below them. For the Analyst, **New thread** and the
  ontology's threads.
- Your account menu, and **Settings**.

A member [restricted to some sources](/foundry/workspace/access#restricted-members) sees
only **Analyst**, **Ontologies** and **Explorer**, with no search or Settings.

Three sections have a second panel, beside the sidebar:

| Section | Its panel |
| --- | --- |
| **Ontologies** | The workspace's ontologies, with the pages of the one open |
| **Explorer** | The labels of the graph, or the types in Records. Press <kbd>[</kbd> to hide it and show it again. |
| **Connectors** | The Connectors explorer: connections and documents as a tree |

## Search with the command palette

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), or
select the search icon, to search pages, connections, synced tables, documents and
ontologies, switch workspace or theme, and reopen what you opened recently. Owners and
admins also get **Add data**, **New API source** and **Create an ontology** there.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/command-palette.png" alt="The command palette searching for cus, listing synced tables and an ontology" />
</Frame>

## Account menu and theme

Select your name at the foot of the sidebar for Settings, Members, Plan & billing with
the workspace's plan, this month's [usage](/foundry/workspace/usage), the theme and
**Sign out**. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**; Foundry keeps the choice in this
browser.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/account-menu.png" alt="The account menu with Settings, Members, Plan & billing, Usage with this month's spend on loads, API sources, questions and run assessments, the theme choices and Sign out" />

## Workspace settings

**Settings** → **General** shows the workspace's name, address, region and your role.
Rename or delete a workspace in the Helix dashboard; Foundry shares it with Helix Cloud.
Settings also holds [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models),
[Members](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles),
[Access](/foundry/workspace/access),
[Plan & billing](/foundry/workspace/plans-and-billing) and
[People enrichment](/foundry/workspace/data-handling#people-enrichment).

## Emails from Foundry

Foundry emails you about your own work, from Helix:

| Email | When |
| --- | --- |
| **… is ready to ask** | Your workspace's [first ontology](/foundry/ontologies/create-an-ontology#create-your-first-ontology) was drafted and loaded. If the load stopped at the month's budget, it says some documents weren't read. |
| **… couldn't be drafted**, **… couldn't be loaded** | Your first ontology's draft or its load failed. It says why, and links to the ontology to try again. |
| **…'s first draft is ready** | Your first ontology was drafted but not loaded, so the draft waits for your review. |
| **Try Foundry on your own data** | A day after you ran the [sample company](/foundry/start-here/sample-company), if the workspace still has no data of its own. At most once. |

Invitations to a workspace come from Helix; see
[Members and roles](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles#invite-people).
