Use this page to sign in, move between workspaces and get around Foundry quickly.
Foundry's workspaces are your Helix Cloud workspaces: the same names, members and
roles.

## Sign in

Continue with Google, GitHub or Microsoft. Foundry signs you in with your Helix
account, so use the same provider you use for Helix Cloud.

Password and single sign-on sign-ins aren't offered on Foundry yet. If your company
signs in with single sign-on, Foundry says so; use the
[Helix dashboard](https://helix-db.com/login) instead. If your email isn't verified
with the provider, verify it there or continue with another provider.

## Choose or create a workspace

With one workspace, Foundry opens it. With several, or none, it opens the workspace
picker. Select a workspace to open it, or **Create workspace** to start one. A new
workspace starts on Foundry Free.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/workspaces.png" alt="The workspace picker listing the user's workspaces with a Create workspace button" />
</Frame>

To switch later, use the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar. Foundry remembers
the workspace you chose in this browser.

A workspace without an ontology opens on **Create your first ontology**, which connects
data and uploads documents in place. Until its first ontology exists, the sidebar holds
only the workspace menu and your account, and Settings is open for the plan.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/onboarding.png" alt="A new workspace on Create your first ontology, with Set up with your agent and the Name step" />
</Frame>

## The main page: Ask

Foundry opens on [Ask](/foundry/ask/ask-questions), on the ontology you last opened,
or the newest. Ask your data a question there, or pick a suggestion; the **Ontology**
picker at the top opens another ontology. Select **helixfoundry** at the top of the
sidebar to come back to it from anywhere.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/home.png" alt="Ask, Foundry's main page: What do you want to know about Customer contracts? with suggested questions, beside the sidebar with Ask, Ontologies, Explorer and Connectors, two threads under New thread, and the month's questions spend" />
</Frame>

## The sidebar

The sidebar is on every page. From the top:

- **helixfoundry**, which opens Ask, and the search button.
- The workspace menu, to switch workspaces.
- The sections, by name: **Ask**, **Ontologies**, **Explorer** and **Connectors**.
- What the open section adds below them. For Ask, **New thread** and the ontology's
  threads, with what the workspace has spent on questions this month at the foot.
- Your account menu, and **Settings**.

Three sections have a second panel, beside the sidebar:

| Section | Its panel |
| --- | --- |
| **Ontologies** | The workspace's ontologies, with the pages of the one open |
| **Explorer** | The labels of the graph, or the types in Records. Press <kbd>[</kbd> to hide it and show it again. |
| **Connectors** | The Connectors explorer: connections and documents as a tree |

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/data/overview.png" alt="The Connectors section: the sidebar, the Connectors explorer panel beside it, and the Connectors overview" />
</Frame>

On a phone, select the menu button at the top of the page. The drawer it opens holds the
sidebar, or, in a section with a panel, a column of section icons and the panel side by
side.

## Search with the command palette

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), or
select the search icon, to search pages, connections, synced tables, documents and
ontologies, switch workspace or theme, and reopen what you opened recently. Owners and
admins also get **Add data**, **New API source** and **Create an ontology** there.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/command-palette.png" alt="The command palette searching for cus, listing synced tables and an ontology" />
</Frame>

## Account menu and theme

Select your name at the foot of the sidebar for Settings, Members, Plan & billing with
the workspace's plan, this month's [usage](/foundry/workspace/usage), the theme and
**Sign out**. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**; Foundry keeps the choice in this
browser.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/account-menu.png" alt="The account menu with Settings, Members, Plan & billing, Usage with this month's spend on loads, API sources and questions, the theme choices and Sign out" />
</Frame>

## Workspace settings

**Settings** → **General** shows the workspace's name, address, region and your role.
Rename or delete a workspace in the Helix dashboard; Foundry shares it with Helix Cloud.
Settings also holds [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models),
[Members](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles) and
[Plan & billing](/foundry/workspace/plans-and-billing).
