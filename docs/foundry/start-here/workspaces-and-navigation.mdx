Use this page to sign in, move between workspaces and get around Foundry quickly.
Foundry's workspaces are your Helix Cloud workspaces: the same names, members and
roles.

## Sign in

Continue with Google, GitHub or Microsoft, or sign in with your email and password.
**Create one** makes a new account, and **Forgot your password?** emails you a reset
link. Foundry signs you in with your Helix account, so use the same sign-in you use for
Helix Cloud.

If your company requires single sign-on, Foundry sends you through it. If your email
isn't verified yet, Foundry emails you a code to enter on **Check your email**.

## Choose or create a workspace

With one workspace, Foundry opens it. With several, or none, it opens the workspace
picker. Select a workspace to open it, or **Create workspace** to start one. A new
workspace starts on Foundry Free.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/workspaces.png" alt="The workspace picker listing the user's workspaces with a Create workspace button" />
</Frame>

To switch later, use the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar. Foundry remembers
the workspace you chose in this browser.

A workspace without an ontology opens on **Get started**: try the
[sample company](/foundry/start-here/sample-company), hand the setup to your agent, or
create the [first ontology](/foundry/ontologies/create-an-ontology#create-your-first-ontology)
yourself. Until then, the sidebar holds only **helixfoundry**, the workspace menu,
**Get started**, **Feedback**, the **Founder** button and your account menu, which offers **Plan & billing**, usage, the theme and **Sign out**;
there's no search or Settings link. Members see **This workspace isn't set up yet**
until an owner or admin creates one.

## The main page: Founder

Foundry opens on Founder, on the ontology you last opened, or the newest. Ask a
question there, or pick a suggestion, and Founder answers with sources cited. The
**Ontology** picker at the top opens another ontology. Select **helixfoundry** at the top
of the sidebar to come back to it from anywhere. See [Founder](/foundry/ask/founder) for
what it can do, and [Ask questions](/foundry/ask/ask-questions) for how to read an answer.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is on every page. From the top:

- **helixfoundry**, which opens Founder, and the search button.
- The workspace menu, to switch workspaces.
- The sections, by name: [**Founder**](/foundry/ask/founder),
  [**Dashboards**](/foundry/automate/dashboards),
  [**Workflows**](/foundry/automate/workflows) and **Ontologies**.
  The Explorer has no link of its own: open it from an ontology in the Ontologies panel.
- What the open section adds below them: for Founder, **New chat**, your threads and any
  **Earlier questions**; for Dashboards, its dashboards; for Workflows, while one is
  open, the workflows.
- Jobs in progress, while there are any, then **Connectors** and **Feedback**.
- Your account menu, the **Founder** button (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
  <kbd>J</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and **Settings**.

A [restricted member](/foundry/workspace/access#restricted-members) sees only
**Founder**, **Dashboards** and **Ontologies**, with the Explorer of the ontologies they
can read. They get no search, Connectors or Settings, and their account menu holds only
the theme and **Sign out**.

Three sections have a second panel, beside the sidebar:

| Section | Its panel |
| --- | --- |
| **Ontologies** | The workspace's ontologies, with the pages of the one open |
| **Explorer** | The labels of the graph, or the types in Records. Press <kbd>[</kbd> to hide it and show it again. |
| **Connectors** | The Connectors explorer: connections and documents as a tree |

## Search with the command palette

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), or
select the search icon, to search pages, connections, synced tables, documents,
ontologies, dashboards and workflows, open Founder, switch workspace or theme, and
reopen what you opened recently. Owners and admins also get **Add data**, **New API
source** and **Create an ontology** there. Restricted members, and a workspace still on
**Get started**, have no search.

  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/start-here/command-palette.png" alt="The command palette searching for cus, listing synced tables and an ontology" />

## Account menu and theme

Select your name at the foot of the sidebar for Settings, Members, Plan & billing with
the workspace's plan, this month's [usage](/foundry/workspace/usage), the theme and
**Sign out**. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**; Foundry keeps the choice in this
browser.

## Workspace settings

**Settings** → **General** shows the workspace's name, address, region and your role.
Rename or delete a workspace in the Helix dashboard; Foundry shares it with Helix Cloud.
Settings also holds [AI models](/foundry/workspace/ai-models),
[Members](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles),
[Access](/foundry/workspace/access) (owners and admins),
[Plan & billing](/foundry/workspace/plans-and-billing), People enrichment (Helix has
paused its lookups for now) and Sharing, for public dashboard links.

## Emails from Foundry

Foundry emails you, from Helix, about your own work:

| Email | When |
| --- | --- |
| **… is ready to ask** | Your workspace's [first ontology](/foundry/ontologies/create-an-ontology#create-your-first-ontology) was drafted and loaded. If the load left some documents unread, because of the month's budget or documents the model couldn't read, it says so and why. |
| **… couldn't be drafted**, **… couldn't be loaded** | Your first ontology's draft or load failed. It says why, and links to the ontology to try again. |
| **…'s first draft is ready** | Your first ontology was drafted but not loaded, so the draft waits for your review. |
| **Try Foundry on your own data** | Once, a day after you ran the [sample company](/foundry/start-here/sample-company), if the workspace still has no data of its own. |
| **… has questions for you** | Foundry's agent, drafting your first ontology, needs a few answers before it can finish. If no one answers within a week, it carries on with its own best guesses. |
| **&lt;workflow&gt;: …** | A [workflow](/foundry/automate/workflows) you own reached a step that emails you what it did, and any actions waiting for you. |
| **… paused after failing** | A workflow you own failed several runs in a row, so Foundry paused it. It says why, and links to the workflow to resume it. |

Invitations to a workspace come from Helix; see
[Members and roles](/foundry/workspace/members-and-roles#invite-people).
