Documents carry what your tables don't: who signed a contract, what was agreed, who
referred whom. Upload them to an ontology, and each load reads them into its database,
linking the names they mention to the records your tables hold.

## Upload

In the ontology's **Documents**, choose files or drop them on the page. Foundry checks
each file before it uploads it, then sends it straight to storage with its progress.

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| File types | Markdown (`.md`, `.markdown`) and text (`.txt`) |
| Size | Up to 256 KiB each |
| Count | At most 200 documents in the workspace |
| Name | At most 255 characters, with no slash, backslash or control character |

## How documents are used

- **Generations** give the agent the names of the ontology's 50 most recently uploaded
  documents, so a draft can add types for what they hold, such as a contract.
- **Loads** read every document: its sections, the names it mentions, and the facts it
  states, each with the quote it came from. What a load can't settle, such as a name
  the database doesn't hold, an ambiguous name, a quote it couldn't verify or a fact
  that conflicts with your tables, goes to **Needs review** instead of becoming a
  record.
- A document uploaded after the current load is read into that load as it arrives.

Each document's status says where it stands: **Waiting for a load**, **Queued for the
load**, **Reading**, **Read**, **Needs review**, **Duplicate** or **Couldn't be read**.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/documents.png" alt="The Documents list with contract.md read and one item needing review" />
</Frame>

Reading documents uses AI models: a load sends each document's text to Anthropic and
OpenAI models to find its names and facts and to check them.

## Read a document

Open a document to see its text as the load read it: names underlined, quoted facts
tinted and anything that needs review marked. Beside it, **Facts**, **Entities** and
**Needs review** list what the load found, each linked to its record.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/document.png" alt="contract.md with names underlined and facts tinted, and its two signed_with facts listed with their quotes" />
</Frame>

Use the **Load** picker to read the document as another load saw it.

## Delete a document

Select **Delete** on a document's page. Owners and admins upload and delete documents;
members read them.
