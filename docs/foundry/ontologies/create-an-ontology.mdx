An ontology holds the types, links and rules that describe your data, with its
versions and loads. Each one is bound to one Helix database, which its loads write
into. A workspace can have several ontologies, one per database, and each reads the
workspace's [data](/foundry/data/connect-data) and
[documents](/foundry/data/documents) its versions pick.

## The Ontologies panel

In **Ontologies**, the panel beside the sidebar lists the workspace's ontologies, each
with its database and a dot for the status of its last load. The open ontology's row
holds its pages: **Overview**, which also lists its schema versions and data loads,
**Documents**, with how many it reads and the sets they're in, and **Workflows**, the
runs your agents record through the [Helix MCP](/foundry/agents/helix-mcp#workflows).
Owners and admins create an ontology with **+** in the panel's head. From eight
ontologies, a filter finds one by name or database.

**Ontologies** in the sidebar opens the ontology you last opened in this browser, or
the first one.

## Create your first ontology

A workspace without an ontology opens on [Get started](/foundry/start-here/quickstart).
There, owners and admins select **Use my own data**, then **Continue**, to open **Your
first ontology**. After the [sample company](/foundry/start-here/sample-company), the
workspace's first ontology of its own starts the same way from **New ontology**.

It has three steps, one at a time:

1. **Connect your data or upload documents**, as [below](#create-another-ontology).
2. **Goal (optional)**: under **What do you want to understand?**, the questions you
   want to answer, up to 2,000 characters. **Skip** leaves it empty.
3. **Review and create** says what happens next. Select **Create and generate**.

You don't name the ontology or choose its database. Foundry:

- names it after what it drafts from, such as "Stripe and Contracts", or "Stripe, Shop,
  Contracts, and 2 more" for more than three sources;
- creates a [new Helix database](#a-new-database) named the same, on Helix's shared
  cluster, in the workspace's first Helix project, or in a new project named after the
  workspace;
- has its agent draft a first version, then makes the draft current and loads it as
  soon as it is stored, without a review;
- emails you when you can ask it questions, or if the draft or the load failed.

The generation's page says **Foundry loads it when it's drafted**, then **Drafted
version 1, now current** while the load runs. You can leave the page. Later versions
wait for your review, as for any ontology.

## Create another ontology

Select **New ontology**, or **+** in the Ontologies panel's head. The page walks through
five steps, one at a time: each step you finish shrinks to a line saying what you chose,
with **Edit** to change it. Select **Continue** to go on, or **Skip** on the optional
goal. Your choices are kept in the browser tab until you create the ontology or select
**Cancel**, and the step is in the page's address, so going back or reloading keeps your
place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name">
    Give the ontology a name, such as "Customer contracts".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Database">
    **Use an existing one** lists the live databases in the workspace's Helix projects.
    A database another ontology uses is greyed out. Loads write beside anything the
    database already holds, and Foundry never deletes it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/new-ontology.png" alt="The New ontology page at its Database step, with the name Orders done and the orders database chosen; contracts and subscriptions are used by other ontologies" />
    </Frame>

    **Create a new one** makes a database in a project you choose, or a new project, on
    Helix's shared cluster, under the workspace's Helix plan. Its address is the name you
    give it, with a short suffix when another database already has it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/new-database.png" alt="The Database step creating a database named orders-graph in the Staging project" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your data or upload documents">
    Choose what the agent drafts from. Any of these will do, and you need at least one:

    - **Tables**: the connectors whose synced tables it reads; every table a chosen
      connector has synced goes in. Connectors are grouped by kind, as in
      [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#generate-a-draft).
    - **API sources**: the [API sources](/foundry/data/api-sources) it reads, each
      through structured mappings or by entity extraction.
    - **Documents**: the [document sets](/foundry/data/documents) the first version
      reads, up to 20.

    A generation reads at most 50 tables, synced tables and table files together. If a
    source isn't connected yet, select **Add data** to connect it without leaving the
    page; its first sync shows in its row, and you can continue once it has synced.
    **New API source** makes an API source in place. **Upload documents** uploads files
    into **General** or the oldest set, and the upload button on a set's row uploads
    into that set; the set the files went into is then chosen. The
    [sample company's](/foundry/start-here/sample-company) connection and set aren't
    offered.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/new-ontology-data.png" alt="The step Connect your data or upload documents, with the Stripe and Shop database connectors and the General document set chosen, Add data and Upload documents" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Goal (optional)">
    Under **What do you want to understand?**, describe the questions you want to answer,
    in your words, up to 2,000 characters. The goal names the generation. **Copy prompt
    for your agent** has your own AI assistant help you write it, as on
    [Generate](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#generate-a-draft).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and create">
    Check what you chose, then select **Create and generate**. Foundry makes the new
    database if you asked for one, creates the ontology, and asks its agent for a first
    draft. The generation's page opens and
    [follows it](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#follow-a-generation).

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/new-ontology-review.png" alt="The Review and create step, with Orders, the orders database, Stripe and Shop database, General and a goal chosen, and Create and generate" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You review the draft before it is used: make it current to load it. If you leave
before then, the ontology's [overview](#the-overview) picks up where you stopped. If
the ontology was created but its generation couldn't start, the ontology opens saying
why, with **Try again**, which opens Generate with your choices.

## A new database

- **Its API key**: a new database comes with a first API key. Foundry never keeps or
  shows its token; right after you create the ontology, the page says so once and links
  to the database's keys in the Helix dashboard, where you can revoke it or make your
  own.
- **Starting**: it takes a few minutes to start. Until it answers, the ontology's pages
  say it is starting and read it again by themselves, and a load waits for it.
- **Plan**: it needs a
  [Helix Cloud plan](/foundry/workspace/plans-and-billing#the-helix-cloud-plan-for-databases)
  on the workspace, free the first time for a workspace that never had one. If the
  workspace's Helix plan has ended, or it signed up through the Helix website, the
  review step says **A new database needs a Helix plan**, with **Open Helix billing**
  and, for an ontology after the first, **Use an existing one**.

## The overview

Until an ontology is set up, its overview carries the setup on, so you can always
continue from there. **Set it up** lists the steps with the ones done ticked: Name and
Database, then **Connect your data or upload documents**, Goal (optional), **Generate a
draft**, **Review and make a version current**, **Load it** and **Explore or ask**. The
first step not done is open, and **Set up with your agent** copies a prompt that carries
on from there:

- Before a draft, the data and goal steps are the same as on New ontology, with **Add
  data** and **Upload documents** in place, then **Generate**.
- While the agent drafts, the step follows it; if the draft failed, it says why, with
  **Try again**.
- A draft to review offers **Review** and **Make current**.
- A current version not loaded yet offers **Load data**.
- Once loaded, **Explore or ask** offers **Open the Explorer** and **Ask a question**,
  which opens the Analyst.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/overview-setup.png" alt="The Renewals overview with Set it up, 5 of 7 required steps done: its name, database, data, goal, draft and current version ticked, Load it open with Version 1 isn't loaded yet and Load data, and Set up with your agent" />
</Frame>

Members see the same steps, without the pickers or anything that changes the
ontology.

Once the ontology has been loaded and explored or asked about, the overview is its
current version as a schema: each type as a card with its key and the table that fills
it, joined by its links. Select a card to open that type; point at a link to read what
it means. Above it, the overview follows a generation or load that is running, and
says when a newer version is ready to review or the current one isn't loaded yet.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://assets.helix-db.com/docs/foundry/ontology/overview.png" alt="The Customer contracts overview, loaded: its database, last load and creation date, and version 3's schema with Contract, Customer and Invoice joined by Signed with and Billed to" />
</Frame>

Its header names the database and its Helix project, the status of the last load and
when the ontology was created. It links to the documents the ontology reads, and offers
**Generate** once the ontology has a draft and, once the current version can be loaded,
**Load data**. Below the schema,
[Schema versions](/foundry/ontologies/generate-and-version#schema-versions) lists every
version and [Data loads](/foundry/ontologies/load-data#manage-loads) the loads kept.

The [Explorer](/foundry/explore/explorer) and the [Analyst](/foundry/ask/ask-questions)
are sections of their own, which open the ontology you last opened.

## Delete an ontology

Select the bin on the overview. Foundry deletes the ontology, its versions and loads,
and, in the background, everything its loads wrote into the database, including links
your own data added to those records. The database and its other data stay, and so do
the workspace's documents. The database can take a new ontology once that removal
finishes.

## Who can do what

Owners and admins create and delete ontologies, and need write access to the
database's Helix project to do so. Members see every ontology. A member who can't read
the database's project sees the ontology without its database's details or contents.
